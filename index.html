About

Working in graphite, on deadline, on instinct.

I'm a visual artist working mainly in pencil and graphite, focused on realistic shading and fine detail. My drawings tend to start from a single reference or a shape I can't stop thinking about, and grow outward from there.

Outside of personal projects, I compete in live art events — where a theme is revealed on the spot and the work has to come together fast. That pressure has shaped how I draw as much as any studio habit has.

Process

How a drawing comes together.

  1. 01

    Reference & theme

    I start from a theme, a feeling, or a reference photo — sometimes given only minutes before drawing starts.

  2. 02

    Light sketch

    A loose outline goes down first, marking proportions and composition before any real detail.

  3. 03

    Base shading

    Broad tonal values come in next, establishing where the light and shadow will sit.

  4. 04

    Detail & texture

    Once the composition holds up, I go back in for fine linework, texture, and the small decisions that finish it.
